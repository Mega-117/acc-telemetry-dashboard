The visibility of laps and sessions to other drivers is part of the service (contract).

Where the data is

We use providers that process the data on our behalf: Google Firebase (account, database, real time) and Cloudflare (lap archive, diagnostics, statistics). They may also be outside the European Union, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses / Data Privacy Framework). We do not sell data and we do not show advertising.

Your choices

  • You choose usage statistics when you register (optional box) and you can change them at any time from the Profile. Laps and sessions are visible to other drivers: to stop that you can delete the account. Technical diagnostics cannot be turned off: they are needed for the program to work.
  • You can delete the account and all its data from the Profile, with "Delete account". Laps already downloaded to your PC stay yours and you delete them yourself. Diagnostic packages and temporary Pitwall data are not linked to your account name and expire on their own (diagnostics after 30 days); to delete them earlier use "Report a problem".

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent: write to info@racercore.app or use "Report a problem" in the program. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it), or with the authority of your country.

Age

The beta is reserved for people aged 14 or over.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version appears here with a new date, and we ask you again in the app when the change is relevant. If this notice is translated, the Italian text prevails.
